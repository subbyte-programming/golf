What does the condition grade mean?

Each grade describes cosmetic condition. The final store should publish a clear grade guide and inspect stock consistently before sale.

Are these new or pre-owned golf balls?

Product listings should specify this clearly. The sample range is presented as inspected golf balls; confirm the exact product condition before purchase.

When will my order ship?

Orders are prepared within 1–2 business days in this storefront concept. Actual delivery times depend on destination and carrier.

How do I track an order?

Open the tracking page and enter your order reference. Live tracking requires a connected order-management service.

Can I return an order?
